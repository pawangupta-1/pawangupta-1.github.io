Building, not just studying

Research tells me what's true. Building things is how I test whether that truth actually helps anyone. This is where the research becomes something people can use.

Project Apnapan

Apnapan — a Hindi word that means belonging — is a humane, evidence-based platform that transforms school culture through belonging-centered design, data-driven insights, and co-created solutions for K-12 education. It's the clearest expression yet of my research thesis: technology should enrich the environment around a person, not just deliver content to them.
