Research

Technology, in service of human potential

My research examines how technologies can be designed and evaluated to foster human development — learning, agency, wellbeing, performance, and potential — by enriching the environments and processes people learn and grow within.

I approach this through education data science: using learning analytics, dashboards, and data-driven systems as tools not just to measure learning, but to understand and strengthen the conditions that make it possible. My work has looked at AI-powered learning analytics dashboards, technology-enabled programs for remote and in-person teaching, and how bureaucratic and organizational systems shape what educational tools actually achieve in practice.

Research threads

  • Human development through technology design
    How systems can be built to enrich agency, wellbeing, and performance rather than simply deliver content.
  • Education data science
    Learning analytics and AI-powered dashboards that make learning environments legible to teachers and researchers.
  • Implementation & belonging in schools
    Why well-designed reforms and tools succeed or falter once they meet real classrooms and school cultures.
